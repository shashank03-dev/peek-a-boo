# Peek-a-Boo privacy policy

_Last updated: 5 October 2026_

Peek-a-Boo ("the app") warns you when someone looks at your phone screen over your shoulder. This
policy explains what the app does with your data. Short version: **everything stays on your phone,
and nothing is collected, sent or shared.**

## What the app uses and why

| What | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Front camera** | To see whether someone other than you is looking at the screen. Frames are analysed in memory, on the device, with Google ML Kit's on-device face detection, and then thrown away. | Nowhere. No video or frames are saved or sent. |
| **Your face signature** (only if you set up "Face ID") | So you're never counted as a peeker. It's a small set of numbers computed from your face, not a photo. | Stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. |
| **Peeker snapshots** (on by default, can be turned off) | A small photo of whoever peeked, so you can see who it was in your report. | Stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. |
| **Peek history** (times, durations, number of people) | To build your daily report. | Stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. |
| **Usage access** (Pro, optional) | To know which app is open, so the Privacy Shield can protect only the apps you choose. | Read on the device and never stored or sent. |
| **Display over other apps** | To show the peek notch, Privacy Shield and Blackout over other apps. | Not applicable. |

## What the app does not do

- The app has **no internet permission**. It cannot send anything anywhere.
- No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party data sharing.
- Face data is never used to identify who someone is. It's only compared against your own enrolled face, on the device.

## Subscriptions

Peek-a-Boo Pro is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment; the app only receives
confirmation from the Google Play app on your phone that your subscription is active. We never see
your payment details. Google's own privacy policy applies to the purchase:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Sharing and export

The app only shares data when you tap **Share** or **Export history** yourself, and then only to
the app you pick in Android's share sheet.

## Deleting your data

- **Settings › Hold to clear history** deletes every recorded peek and snapshot.
- **You › Remove face** deletes your face signature.
- Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
