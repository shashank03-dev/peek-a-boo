**App name** (30 max)
Peek-a-Boo: Privacy Screen

**Short description** (80 max)
Catch shoulder surfers. Auto privacy screen hides your phone when someone looks.

**Full description**

Someone reading your chats on the bus? Peek-a-Boo notices.

Peek-a-Boo uses your front camera to spot when someone other than you is looking at your screen. The
moment they do, a little "Peeping · 1" notch drops out of your camera and your phone gives a soft buzz.
It's private by design: everything runs on your phone and the app doesn't even have internet access.

👀 CATCH PEEKERS
• Real-time peek detection: only counts people who are actually looking at your screen, not passers-by
• A Dynamic-Island-style notch that works over any app
• Face ID for you: you're never counted, even when you lean in next to a friend
• A daily report: peeks, how many people, longest stare, busiest hour, and snapshots of who it was

🛡️ PEEK-A-BOO PRO
• Privacy Shield: the screen darkens and scrambles automatically when someone looks. It's like a
  privacy screen protector that only switches on when you need it. Pick Louver, Dim or Grain.
• Protected apps: keep the shield on just for banking, chats and photos
• Someone-else alert: if someone else is using your unlocked phone, it's quietly logged with a photo

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No internet permission. Nothing ever leaves your phone.
• No ads, no accounts, no tracking
• The camera only runs while your phone is unlocked, and stops on lock

Pro is a subscription with a free trial. Cancel anytime in Google Play.

Note: a phone screen can't physically narrow its viewing angle like a hardware privacy display. The
Privacy Shield cuts contrast and adds a fine pattern so text is hard to read from beside you, while
you can still read it head-on.

Permissions: Camera (detect peekers), Display over other apps (notch, shield), Notifications (guard
status), Usage access (optional, Pro protected apps).
